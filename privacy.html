Privacy Policy

Polar H10 Acquisition and PARAM H10 Companion
Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Polar H10 Acquisition (desktop) and PARAM H10 Companion (Android) handle data, including when you connect your own Google Drive account to upload research files.

Who we are

These research tools are developed at NIMHANS, Bengaluru, India, for recording Polar H10 heart-rate, ECG and acceleration data in research studies. Source code is available at github.com/hollabharath/PolarH10-Tools.

Data the apps handle

  • Recording data: ECG, RR intervals, heart rate, acceleration, event markers, and session details you enter (participant ID, session, task, and optionally name, age and sex).
  • Google account access (optional): if you choose to upload to Google Drive, you sign in with your own Google account and authorize access to a Drive folder.

Where your data is stored

Recording files are saved on your own devices (your phone and your computer). The apps do not send recording data to any server run by the developers. Data moves only where you direct it: from the phone to your computer over your local network, and from your computer to the cloud storage you configure.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the tools use the Google Drive API only to upload the recording files you select into the Drive folder you choose. We do not read, copy or analyze any other content in your Drive.

  • What is accessed: the destination Drive folder and the files uploaded to it.
  • How it is used: solely to copy your research files to your own Drive so you can store and share them with your team.
  • Where credentials are kept: the authorization token is stored locally on your computer in your own configuration file. It is never sent to the developers.
  • No sharing or sale: Google user data is not transferred to the developers or any third party, not sold, and not used for advertising.
  • No human access: the developers have no access to your Google account, tokens or Drive content.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Because data lives on your devices and in your own cloud account, you control retention. You can delete recordings from your phone, computer or Drive folder at any time. To remove the apps' Google access, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local configuration file that holds the token.

Research participants

If you use these tools to collect data from people, you are responsible for obtaining consent, applying ethics approval, and storing the data in line with your institution's policies and applicable law. We recommend using coded participant IDs rather than names, and limiting cloud access to a dedicated study folder.

Security

Treat your configuration file like a password: it can contain credentials that allow access to your cloud storage. Do not share it by email, chat or version control.

Children

The apps are intended for use by researchers and are not directed at children. Any data from minor participants must be handled under your study's ethics approval and guardian consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the tools change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
